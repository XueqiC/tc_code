## 6. Controls / interpretation

* gentle vs lrmatch share target masses, Λ and the capped duals; only the optimiser differs → the gentle arm's +0.9 is optimiser drift that moved the call/abstain boundary (Rel +12.5 / Irrel −20.7), not atom mastery (0/32 satisfied, J +0.03 in both). lrmatch = base ± noise = stability without learning. Per §8.1: lr is the dominant factor of the collapse; the "gentle bundle" is not a distillation success; pause new pressure scans.
