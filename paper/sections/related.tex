\paragraph{Distilling black-box teachers.}
Sequence-level knowledge distillation trains a student on teacher
outputs \citep{kim2016sequence, mukherjee2023orca}, and its agentic
descendants refine what is imitated, whether with segment-specific
losses over reasoning and action spans \citep{liu2025structured},
retrospective thought prefixes \citep{kang2025distilling},
capacity-aligned trajectory selection \citep{tang2026smartad}, teacher
feedback turned into preference objectives \citep{tunstall2023zephyr,
pbsd2026}, or teacher relabeling of student-visited states
\citep{ye2025bbopd}. The limits of imitation are documented. Small
models imitating proprietary teachers acquire style rather than
capability \citep{gudibande2023false}, fine-tuning erases previously
held abilities \citep{luo2023empirical}, and agent-tuning pipelines
mix general data into training precisely to limit that erosion
\citep{zeng2023agenttuning}. These observations are treated as
separate pathologies to be patched. We measure them jointly as the
task-extraneous component of the supervision-induced update and remove
them at the source by never using teacher tokens as targets.

\paragraph{Self-improvement with verifiers.}
A complementary line trains models on their own verified outputs.
STaR retrains on self-generated solutions that pass a checker
\citep{zelikman2022star}, rejection-sampling fine-tuning and ReST
generalize this into generate-filter-train loops
\citep{yuan2023scaling, gulcehre2023rest, singh2023beyond}, self-play
uses earlier checkpoints as opponents \citep{chen2024spin}, and online
preference optimization shows that fresh negatives beat frozen ones
\citep{guo2024direct}. Context distillation compresses in-context
information into weights \citep{snell2022learning}, and
self-distillation fine-tuning has the student rewrite training data in
its own words to bridge distribution gaps \citep{yang2024self}. These
methods either assume the model can already sample successes or take
the data as given. Our setting has neither, since the cold student
almost never succeeds unaided and every demonstration costs money. The
scaffold is what connects the two lines, as purchased teacher context
raises the student's success rate by an order of magnitude, and the
verifier-filtered self-training then proceeds on the student's own
trajectories with importance correction toward the deployment policy.

\paragraph{Budgeted acquisition and certified deployment.}
Gradient features locate influential or task-relevant data
\citep{koh2017understanding, pruthi2020estimating, xia2024less,
park2023trak}, and sparse decompositions of gradients expose reusable
behavior directions \citep{olshausen1996emergence, rosser2026gradient}.
Data-selection methods built on such signals curate a fixed corpus
\citep{engstrom2024dsdm, gist2026, trim2025, icons2025}, whereas our
acquisition prices each candidate purchase by its expected unlocked
coverage of a task-demand subspace per teacher token and stops
spending endogenously, with budgeted maximization grounded in
submodular coverage \citep{khuller1999budgeted, krause2005near}.
Active learning likewise selects queries to label under a budget
\citep{settles2009active}, but its labels are targets, whereas our
purchases are contexts. On the deployment side, split conformal
prediction supplies distribution-free acceptance regions
\citep{vovk2005algorithmic, angelopoulos2023conformal}, and serving
gates apply related ideas to reject out-of-scope requests
\citep{li2026scope}. We calibrate the boundary on a split that touches
no training decision, so the certificate applies to the same student
it routes.
