\section{Experimental setup}\label{sec:expsetting}
%% One paragraph per component (PI structure). Solid, aligned with recent
%% agent-distillation practice; no em-dashes; verified citations only.

\paragraph{Datasets and benchmarks.}
Mechanistic experiments use a controlled five-domain suite built from public
datasets: GSM8K \citep{cobbe2021gsm8k} in two action spaces (mechanically
derived, execution-verified Python solutions and the original
chain-of-thought solutions, which form an identical-query hard pair),
DS-1000 Pandas \citep{lai2023ds1000}, Spider text-to-SQL \citep{yu2018spider},
and Alpaca instructions \citep{taori2023alpaca} as the out-of-domain pool
(120 examples per domain, sha256-stable splits). Teacher trajectory pools are
generated by three current open teachers with execution-based quality gates
(95--96\% verified on GSM8K-code; 786 verified trajectories, the third
teacher contributing GSM8K-code only). Agent-level
evaluation uses environments standard in recent tool-use and
agent-distillation work: AppWorld \citep{trivedi2024appworld} as the primary
code-acting environment, BFCL~v4 for function calling, $\tau^2$-bench
\citep{yao2024taubench} for tool-agent-user domains, and ALFWorld
\citep{shridhar2021alfworld} with WebShop \citep{yao2022webshop} for direct
comparability with agent-distillation baselines that report on them
\citep{liu2025structured, kang2025distilling}.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
Selection baselines under matched token budgets: the full pool, uniform
random subsets, embedding-based selection (BGE retrieval scores), and
LESS-style gradient selection targeted at a validation set
\citep{xia2024less}, which isolates our contribution of selecting toward a
\emph{specification} rather than a validation set. Distillation baselines:
vanilla trajectory SFT, capacity-aligned trajectory selection
\citep{tang2026smartad}, and segment-weighted losses
\citep{liu2025structured}. Boundary baselines: query- and
trajectory-embedding subspaces under the same conformal pipeline, and
activation-probe rejection in the style of serving-time gates
\citep{li2026scope}. All baselines share students, budgets, and verifiers
with our method.

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
Both sides of the alignment objective are measured behaviorally. Coverage is
execution-verified success on held-out in-domain tasks; leakage is retained
out-of-domain capability (execution or task-appropriate validity on held-out
domains) together with behavioral channels (action-space adoption, format
bleed, refusal rates). Per-environment metrics follow each benchmark's convention (execution
accuracy for GSM8K-style tasks, task success rate for AppWorld and ALFWorld,
score for WebShop, tool-call accuracy for BFCL, pass\^{}k-style task success
for $\tau^2$-bench), with a macro average across environments as the
headline number, matching recent agent-distillation reporting
\citep{kang2025distilling, liu2025structured}. All evaluation decoding is
greedy (pass@1) with a 320-token generation cap. Point estimates carry
mean$\pm$std over three training seeds with per-task paired comparisons; deployment-facing results are
additionally reported as distribution-free certificates: Clopper--Pearson
lower bounds on coverage and upper bounds on leakage at $\delta{=}0.1$,
following conformal risk practice \citep{vovk2005algorithmic,
gibbs2025conditional}. Boundary quality is reported as AUROC plus conformal
coverage and false-accept rates at $\alpha{=}0.1$.

\paragraph{Implementation and settings.}
Students span Qwen3.5 0.8B--27B in base and instruct variants plus a 35B
mixture-of-experts architecture control; all training uses LoRA
\citep{hu2022lora} (r{=}16, $\alpha{=}32$, all projection modules) with
size-scaled learning rates and, where stated, absorption-triggered stopping
derived from the demand instrumentation of Section~\ref{sec:capability}.
Gradient features use the zero-initialized adapter sketch with per-module
Johnson--Lindenstrauss projection to 64 dimensions
\citep{johnson1984extensions}; dictionaries use 128 atoms
(mini-batch dictionary learning, $\alpha{=}0.05$). Teacher pools are sampled once at temperature 0 with anti-degeneracy
prompting and execution filtering; per-teacher trajectory counts and token
statistics are logged in the released manifests. Generated code executes in
a resource-limited subprocess sandbox; token budgets are 10k--40k response
tokens; conformal calibration uses 10--15 held-out specification examples.
Optimization uses AdamW, batch size 1 with gradient accumulation 8, and
3 epochs unless absorption-triggered stopping ends training earlier.
Experiments ran on one lab GPU node and a SLURM cluster of NVIDIA B200
accelerators; all runs are configuration-driven with logged manifests.
