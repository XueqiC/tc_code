\section{Experimental setup}\label{sec:expsetting}
%% Main-experiment setup only; full details in Appendix~\ref{app:details}.

\paragraph{Tasks and protocol.}
Main experiments instantiate the setting of Section~\ref{sec:setting} on
a controlled suite built from public datasets (GSM8K code-acting
\citep{cobbe2021gsm8k} as the primary task; Spider text-to-SQL
\citep{yu2018spider} scoped to a subtopic for boundary stress tests) and
on AppWorld \citep{trivedi2024appworld} as the agent environment. Every
method receives the same support set ($k{=}50$), the same single
pay-per-token teacher, and the same budget $b$; no method sees a
pre-existing corpus, and every demonstration token is charged. In-task
accuracy is execution-verified on the official GSM8K test split (200
held-out questions, disjoint from all training material); boundary
control is measured as off-task residue on the scoped-subtopic task.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
Published acquisition baselines re-implemented faithfully under the
metered protocol: Self-Instruct \citep{wang2023selfinstruct},
Evol-Instruct \citep{xu2024wizardlm}, LLM2LLM \citep{lee2024llm2llm},
and AlpaGasus \citep{chen2024alpagasus}; selection baselines LESS
\citep{xia2024less} and embedding retrieval under matched budgets. All
baselines share students, budgets, support sets, and verifiers with our
method.

\paragraph{Evaluation.}
The primary metrics are execution-verified in-task accuracy, off-task
residue, wasted-budget fraction, and budget-to-target $B^{*}(\tau)$;
point estimates carry mean$\pm$std over three seeds. Deployment results
add distribution-free certificates (Clopper--Pearson bounds at
$\delta{=}0.1$; conformal coverage at $\alpha{=}0.1$).

\paragraph{Implementation.}
Students are Qwen3.5 (2B main; 0.8B--9B for scale analysis) trained with
LoRA (r{=}16) under compute-matched SFT; gradient features use
per-module Johnson--Lindenstrauss projections \citep{johnson1984extensions};
dictionaries use mini-batch sparse coding ($\alpha{=}0.05$). Budgets span
2k--40k response tokens. All runs are configuration-driven with logged
manifests; hyperparameters, environment details, secondary benchmarks,
and teacher-pool statistics appear in Appendix~\ref{app:details}.
