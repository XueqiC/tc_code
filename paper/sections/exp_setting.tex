\paragraph{Benchmarks, baselines, and evaluation.}
We evaluate on four agent benchmarks under their official harnesses
and metrics, namely \textbf{AppWorld} \citep{trivedi2024appworld}
(unit-test pass ratio), \textbf{BFCL~v4} (overall accuracy),
\textbf{$\tau^2$-bench} \citep{yao2024taubench}
(pass\textsuperscript{1}), and \textbf{ALFWorld}
\citep{shridhar2021alfworld} (unseen-split success rate); benchmark
details are given in Appendix~\ref{app:details}.

Following the black-box branches of the distillation taxonomy of
\citet{xu2024kdsurvey}, the baselines are \emph{Vanilla SFT}
\citep{mukherjee2023orca}, \emph{Structured Agent Distillation}
\citep{liu2025structured}, the agent distiller of
\citet{kang2025distilling}, \emph{dDPO} \citep{tunstall2023zephyr},
\emph{PBSD} \citep{pbsd2026}, \emph{STaR} \citep{zelikman2022star},
and \emph{Black-Box On-Policy Distillation} \citep{ye2025bbopd}, all
consuming the same budget from the same teacher. Every number is
mean$\pm$std over three seeds with the change against the untrained
base student explicit; budget efficiency and off-task residue metrics
are defined in Appendix~\ref{app:details}.

\paragraph{Few-shot setup.}
Each benchmark instantiates Definition~\ref{def:setting}
independently, following the support and query division of classical
few-shot evaluation \citep{vinyals2016matching, snell2017prototypical}.
The support set $\mathcal{S}$ is drawn once with a fixed seed,
stratified over task categories, from the official training split when
one exists and from the full task set otherwise, and all methods,
including the base student, are evaluated on the query side only. The
support size is five percent of the pool clipped to $[50,250]$,
matching demonstration volumes in agent distillation work
\citep{kang2025distilling, liu2025structured}; eighty percent of
$\mathcal{S}$ is the working split $\mathcal{S}_d$ and the remainder
is the calibration split $\mathcal{S}_c$, with per-benchmark sizes in
Table~\ref{tab:support-sizes}. The budget $B$ counts every teacher
output token including discarded failures, teacher-using methods are
matched at the same $B$, and teacher-free baselines follow their
native zero-teacher protocol.

\paragraph{Implementation details.}
All main-text experiments use one fixed pair, a metered black-box
teacher (DeepSeek-V4-Pro via API, exposing neither logits nor hidden
states nor gradients) and a Qwen3.5-4B student; other student scales
appear in Appendix~\ref{app:details}. Students train with LoRA
adapters (rank 16) under compute-matched schedules, and every
threshold in the method is either a statistical convention or
estimated from the support set, with all constants listed in
Appendix~\ref{app:details}. Experiments run on a local server with
five mixed NVIDIA GPUs (48--96GB) for simulator-bound evaluation and
on NVIDIA B200 nodes for training sweeps; one method on one benchmark
takes two to five GPU hours at 4B.
