\subsection{Does measured capability track behavior?}\label{sec:capability}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/e2_gate.png}
\caption{The failed pre-registered gate (pilot). Left: mean residual
gradient norm and execution success across 66 optimizer steps; the norm
falls, then rises as the student's own style diverges from teacher
references while success is stable. Right: success rate by residual-norm
decile; the lowest decile is highly predictive (96\% success) but the
upper deciles are not, and the pooled correlation ($\rho=-0.40$) fails the
$-0.5$ threshold.}
