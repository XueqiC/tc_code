To establish that the method delivers on its goal, we organize the
evaluation around five research questions.
\textbf{RQ1} asks whether the full method improves few-shot agent
specialization under a common teacher budget cap. \textbf{RQ2} asks
whether the return-chosen replacement beats the uniform transfer at
equal teacher mass on identical purchased data. \textbf{RQ3} asks how
much acquisition by the joint-update surrogate contributes when the
training procedure is held fixed. \textbf{RQ4} asks how much the
distillation procedure contributes on identical purchased data.
\textbf{RQ5} asks which components are necessary and how efficiently
the teacher budget is converted into task capability. The following
subsections answer these questions in turn.

\subsection{RQ2, does the return-chosen replacement beat the uniform transfer}
\label{sec:rq2}
The mechanism claim of Section~\ref{sec:distill} is that, at equal
teacher mass, choosing which student behavior gives way by the
learning return improves on replacing both uniformly. We test it with
paired interventions on frozen checkpoints and fixed window evidence
(Appendix~\ref{app:details}): the learned $d^\star$ against $d=0$
with the same $\alpha$, weights, teacher records, and source samples;
the same update with $Y_1$ and $Y_2$ swapped, which must flip the
sign of $d$ and leave the update unchanged; and the learned $d$
against a shuffled source pairing that keeps the multiset of gate
values and teacher targets. \todo{fill from the embedded paired
controls of the V2 windows.} The test is falsifiable in both
directions, since a realized gain indistinguishable from the shuffled
pairing would show that the selection carries no information beyond
the injection, and a negative correlation between $\hat z$ and the
realized gain would refute the surrogate.

\subsection{RQ4, value of the training channel on identical data}
\label{sec:rq4}
Table~\ref{tab:distill} feeds the identical purchased evidence to
every distillation baseline of Table~\ref{tab:main} and to ours, so
whatever difference remains can only come from the training
procedure. The fixed-purchased-data control of the protocol serves the
same purpose in the other direction: it trains our procedure on the
random control arm's purchases. \todo{fill from the distillation
matrix on the frozen purchased set.} The comparison the mechanism
predicts is that procedures that replace the student's behaviors
wholesale lose part of what the initial student could do before they
finish absorbing the teacher, while the transfer with fixed teacher
mass and a return-chosen replacement keeps it.
